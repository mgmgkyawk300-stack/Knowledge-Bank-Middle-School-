Who we are

About Our School

Knowledge Bank Middle School aims to provide students with a supportive environment to learn, grow and prepare for the future. We value education, respect, responsibility and cooperation.

Our school community works together to encourage curiosity, confidence and lifelong learning.

Our Mission

To provide meaningful learning opportunities and help students develop the knowledge, skills and values needed to contribute positively to their families and communities.

Our Vision

A community of confident, responsible and compassionate learners who can build a brighter future through education.
